## Observed Feasibility Pair

The owner-local eeBUS read selected the privately bound VR940
`Measurement/measurementListData` field whose protected descriptor identified
`measurementId=0`, `outsideAirTemperature`, and `degC`. The value was `13 degC`.
The operator view represented it as `number=13`, `scale=0`, and
`valueType=value`. The associated declared step was `0.5 degC`, with observed
minimum `-60 degC` and maximum `80 degC`; this record does not reproduce the
complete typed descriptor or raw response.
The public record does not retain the peer identity, SPINE device address,
native service/entity/feature selectors, raw response, or selector remapping.

The eBUS observation selected B524
`OP=0x02/GG=0x00/II=0x00/RR=0x0073`, category `STATE`, decoded as `f32` in
`degC`. `ebus.v1.runtime.status.get` and the bus logs identified the admitted
initiator as `0x7F` for this observation and the BASV2 target as `0x15`. The
value was `12.66796875 degC`.

The readings were observed seconds apart. Their absolute difference is
`0.33203125 degC`, which is inside the candidate comparator's proposed
equivalence window derived from the source's declared `0.5 degC` step. This
numerical proximity only demonstrates that the pairing is worth a synchronized
experiment. It establishes no metrological accuracy, calibration, or physical
sensor-error bound.
