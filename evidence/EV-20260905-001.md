# Public Catalogue Evidence for the STD-01 Source Ledger

- Evidence id: `EV-20260905-001`
- Evidence class: `vendor_public`
- Capture date: 2026-09-05
- Device family: not applicable
- Firmware/app/runtime versions: not applicable
- Acquisition method: read the public EEBUS catalogue, solution matrix, and terms-of-use pages without authentication
- Artifact class: public web pages
- Private artifact retained: no
- Redaction mode: not applicable
- Publication status: publishable

The public [Specifications & Media catalogue](https://www.eebus.org/specifications-media/)
names SHIP, SPINE, and the E-Mobility, Grid Connection Point, Inverter, and
HVAC use-case collections as available documents, but says registration is
required to download the current versions. The page does not disclose an exact
revision for any of those collections to an unauthenticated reader.

The public [solution matrix](https://www.eebus.org/solutions/) identifies the
relevant use cases and marks them as standardised, released, release candidate,
or in progress. This is catalogue maturity, not a substitute for an exact
document revision or conformance statement.

The public [terms of use](https://www.eebus.org/terms-of-use/) state that the
specifications and tools are copyright-protected, allow informational use and
products based on them, prohibit altered or misleading dissemination, and
restrict other use or exploitation unless expressly permitted. This record does
not reproduce any specification content.
