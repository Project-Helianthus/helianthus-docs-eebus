## Preserved Baseline

The pre-erratum inventory declared 49 READ operations: 26 succeeded and 23
failed. These counts preserve the baseline only. They do not identify a device,
feature, SPINE address, selector, request, response, error text, payload, or
individual failing function.

The 26 successes are not proof that every unreported model shape is correct.
The 23 failures are not proof that any particular upstream correction is the
cause. The aggregate is a regression comparison point for the bounded
candidate, not a protocol-conformance result.

## Operator And Public Boundary

Owner-authorized raw investigation may retain operational observations only at
the existing raw/operator boundary. Public artifacts retain this count summary
and no stable identity, raw request, raw response, error payload, or topology
content. The retained private artifact remains owner-only and outside this
public repository. This evidence does not change the existing raw/operator
versus public-redacted rules.

## Falsification Criteria

A retained redacted summary with a different declared, successful, or failed
count falsifies this baseline. A public export containing an identity, address,
payload, or error body would also falsify this page's redaction classification.
