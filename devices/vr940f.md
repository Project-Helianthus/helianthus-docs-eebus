# Vaillant VR940f / myVaillant eeBUS Target

## Purpose

VR940f/myVaillant is the first Helianthus eeBUS lab target. The first milestone
is raw visibility: sessions, services, topology, pairing state, and raw
evidence through read-only MCP.

## Non-Goals

- No GraphQL exposure beyond the fields named in the inbound heating mapping.
- No Portal consumer workflow.
- No Home Assistant entities.
- No command routing.

## Evidence To Capture

- runtime status;
- local and remote identity with redaction;
- pairing state;
- sessions;
- visible and paired services;
- entity and feature graph;
- usecase claims;
- degraded states;
- restart and reconnect behavior.

## Outdoor-Temperature Candidate

The first read-only promotion experiment targets the candidate leaf
`/system/outdoor_temperature`. The owner-authorized feasibility read observed:

| Feature type and role | `Measurement`, `server`; native selector retained in the private capture |
| Descriptor function | `measurementDescriptionListData` |
| Value function | `measurementListData` |
| Measurement identity | `measurementId=0` |
| Scope and unit | `outsideAirTemperature`, `degC` |
| Operator value representation | `number=13`, `scale=0`, `valueType=value` |
| Observed range and step | minimum `{number:-6,scale:1}` (`-60 degC`), maximum `{number:8,scale:1}` (`80 degC`), step `{number:5,scale:-1}` (`0.5 degC`) |

[`EV-20260811-001`](../evidence/EV-20260811-001.md) records a feasibility
observation for this source and its exact eBUS B524 peer. The two readings were
made seconds apart but were not acquired by the final synchronized contract.
They identify a candidate comparison. That live read is a recorded verification
level (run, not run, result) and conditions nothing. Capture, redaction,
comparator, and dossier design remain in the linked candidate architecture page.

## Prior Partial Observation

`EV-20260720-001` is a redacted partial/negative observation for protected
pairing. It observed the local first-trust registration transition with
automatic acceptance disabled, but did not observe a completed candidate,
SHIP session, OOB confirmation, durable trust write, or trusted reconnect. It
therefore establishes no remote reachability or observed runtime state and does
not complete the live gate below.

## Live Validation

The [canonical live validation gate](../evidence/ship-identity-live-validation.md)
binds the single local advertisement, protected expected-peer comparison,
transport ordering, callback provenance, and restart behavior into one
redacted run. The gate retains no private address, literal peer fingerprint,
packet capture, raw identity, or promoted semantic.

The identity and TXT behavior is normative derived design. Live validation is
pending, and this page does not claim deployed support.

## Inbound heating mapping

Declared from HVAC Temperature 1.0.0 and HVAC System Function 1.0.0. A
temperature read is degree Celsius on the bus and kelvin in SemReg: the stored
value is the Celsius reading plus 273.15. Measurements are read. A setpoint or
mode is also written where that use case marks the element changeable.
Here, declared cross-protocol leaves are promoted and exposed by default. The
checked-in profile promotes every declared leaf and no check fails on a
promoted count. A live coexistence or capture result is a recorded verification
level (run, not run, result) that conditions nothing. A selector is bound from
the published specification and runtime identification, and not from private
installation evidence.

Any number of rooms and zones is supported. The n-th room by entity address
and the n-th heating zone by entity address are each zone n on the native
typed surface. That ordering does not join a room bundle to a heating-zone
bundle.

| use-case value | SPINE feature and scope | SemReg element | subject | conversion to kelvin or SI | GraphQL field 0.6 filled |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hot-water temperature | Measurement, scope `dhwTemperature` (Monitoring of DHW Temperature 1.0.0 scenario 1) | `thermal.measurement.dhw_temperature` | `resource.domestic_hot_water` | Celsius plus 273.15 is kelvin | `Dhw.currentTempC` |
| hot-water setpoint | Setpoint, scope `dhwTemperature` (Configuration of DHW Temperature 1.0.0 scenario 1; read and write) | `thermal.setpoint.dhw_temperature_setpoint` | `resource.domestic_hot_water` | Celsius plus 273.15 is kelvin | `Dhw.targetTempC` |
| one-time hot-water charge | HVAC overrun `oneTimeDhw` (Configuration of DHW System Function 1.0.0 scenarios 2 and 3; read, and write of active or inactive) | `thermal.overrun.one_time_dhw` | `resource.thermal_system` | symbol; not a temperature | not filled |
| outdoor temperature | Measurement, scope `outsideAirTemperature` (Monitoring of Outdoor Temperature 1.0.0 scenario 1) | `thermal.measurement.outdoor_air_temperature` | `resource.thermal_system` | Celsius plus 273.15 is kelvin | `SystemState.outdoorTemperature` |
| room temperature, zone n | Measurement, scope `roomAirTemperature` (Monitoring of Room Temperature 1.0.0 scenario 1) | `thermal.measurement.air_temperature` | `resource.zone` | Celsius plus 273.15 is kelvin | `ZoneState.currentTempC` |
| room setpoint, zone n | Setpoint, scope `roomAirTemperature` (Configuration of Room Heating Temperature 1.0.0 scenario 1; read and write) | `thermal.setpoint.temperature_setpoint` | `resource.zone` | Celsius plus 273.15 is kelvin | `ZoneConfig.targetTempC` |
| room mode, zone n | HVAC `currentOperationModeId` of the heating system function (Configuration of Room Heating System Function 1.0.0 scenario 1; read, and write when the device allows the change) | `thermal.mode.zone` | `resource.zone` | symbol | `ZoneConfig.operatingMode` |

Values with no SemReg element yet stay native in the eebusreg namespace until
QQ11 and QQ14 add their elements: hot-water mode, mode-changeable flags,
gateway brand and vendor, and zone names.
